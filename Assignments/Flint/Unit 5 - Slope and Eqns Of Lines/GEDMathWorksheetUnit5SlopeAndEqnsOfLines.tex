\section*{Part E: Real-World Applications of Slope (Questions 34-38)}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{33}

\item A wheelchair ramp rises 2 feet over a horizontal distance of 24 feet. What is the slope of the ramp?

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A staircase has steps that rise 7 inches for every 10 inches of horizontal distance. What is the slope of the staircase?

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A road elevation increases 300 feet for every 2000 feet of horizontal distance. Express this as a slope in simplest form.

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A mountain trail descends 400 feet in elevation over a horizontal distance of 1 mile (5280 feet). What is the slope of the trail?

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A ladder leans against a wall, reaching 12 feet high. The base of the ladder is 5 feet from the wall. What is the slope of the ladder?

\vspace{1.2cm}

\end{enumerate}
