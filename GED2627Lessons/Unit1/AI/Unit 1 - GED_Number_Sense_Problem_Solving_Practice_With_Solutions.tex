\section*{Practice Questions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Convert \(0.625\) to a fraction in simplest form.
  \item Simplify the fraction \(\frac{84}{126}\).
  \item Round \(7{,}495\) to the nearest hundred.
  \item Round \(0.01986\) to the nearest thousandth.
  \item Classify each number as integer, rational, or irrational (choose all that apply): \(-12,\ \sqrt{2},\ 0.\overline{3},\ \pi\).
  \item Estimate \(398 + 603\) using rounding.
  \item Estimate \(51\times 19\) using compatible numbers.
  \item Approximate \(5{,}982 \div 29\) using friendly numbers.
  \item Write the ratio of 18 students to 12 students in simplest form.
  \item Solve the proportion \(\dfrac{x}{15} = \dfrac{4}{5}\).
  \item Find \(17\%\) of \(260\).
  \item The price of a jacket increases from \(\$48\) to \(\$60\). What is the percent increase?
  \item A pair of shoes costs \(\$85\) and is discounted \(30\%\). After the discount, \(8\%\) sales tax is added. What is the total price?
  \item A recipe uses \(3.6\) liters of lemonade to serve \(9\) people. How many liters are needed to serve \(15\) people (same rate)?
  \item Solve for \(x\): \(3(x-4)+5=20\).
  \item A cyclist travels at an average speed of \(12\) mph for \(1.75\) hours. How far did they travel?
  \item Pump A can fill a tank in \(8\) hours, and Pump B can fill it in \(12\) hours. Working together, how long will it take to fill the tank?
  \item A solution contains \(25\%\) salt by volume. How much water must be added to \(200\ \text{mL}\) of this solution to obtain a \(10\%\) salt solution? (Assume volumes are additive.)
  \item You work \(38\) hours at \(\$14\) per hour. After a \(12\%\) deduction, what is your take-home pay?
  \item A muffin recipe needs \(2.5\) cups of flour to make \(16\) muffins. How many cups are needed to make \(24\) muffins (same recipe)?
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Full Solutions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \(0.625 = \frac{625}{1000} = \frac{125}{200} = \frac{25}{40} = \boxed{\frac{5}{8}}\).
  \item \(\gcd(84,126)=42\). \(\frac{84}{126}=\frac{84\div 42}{126\div 42}=\boxed{\frac{2}{3}}\).
  \item Nearest hundred: the tens digit is \(9\) (round up). \(\boxed{7{,}500}\).
  \item Thousandth place is the third decimal digit. \(0.019\underline{8}6\) has \(8\) in the next place, so round up to \(\boxed{0.020}\).
  \item \(-12\): integer and rational. \(\sqrt{2}\): irrational. \(0.\overline{3}=\frac{1}{3}\): rational. \(\pi\): irrational. \\
  \(\boxed{\text{Ans: } -12\in \mathbb{Z}\cap\mathbb{Q};\ \sqrt{2},\pi\ \text{irrational};\ 0.\overline{3}\in \mathbb{Q}.}\)
  \item \(398\approx 400,\ 603\approx 600\Rightarrow 400+600=\boxed{1000}\). (Exact sum \(=1001\).)
  \item Compatible numbers: \(51\approx 50,\ 19\approx 20\Rightarrow 50\times 20=\boxed{1000}\). (Exact product \(=969\).)
  \item Friendly numbers: \(5{,}982\approx 6{,}000,\ 29\approx 30\Rightarrow 6{,}000\div 30=\boxed{200}\). (Exact quotient \(=206.275\ldots\))
  \item \(18:12=\frac{18}{12}=\frac{3}{2}\Rightarrow \boxed{3:2}\).
  \item Cross-multiply: \(5x=4\cdot 15=60\Rightarrow x=\boxed{12}\).
  \item \(0.17\times 260=\frac{17}{100}\times 260=\frac{17\times 260}{100}= \frac{4420}{100}=\boxed{44.2}\).
  \item Change \(=60-48=12\). Percent increase \(=\frac{12}{48}=0.25=\boxed{25\%}\).
  \item Discounted price \(=85(1-0.30)=85(0.70)=\$59.50\). Tax \(=0.08\times 59.50=\$4.76\). Total \(=59.50+4.76=\boxed{\$64.26}\).
  \item Rate \(=\frac{3.6}{9}=0.4\ \text{L/person}\). For \(15\) people: \(0.4\times 15=\boxed{6\ \text{L}}\).
  \item \(3(x-4)+5=20\Rightarrow 3x-12+5=20\Rightarrow 3x-7=20\Rightarrow 3x=27\Rightarrow x=\boxed{9}\).
  \item \(d=rt=12\times 1.75=\boxed{21\ \text{miles}}\).
  \item Rates add: \(\frac{1}{8}+\frac{1}{12}=\frac{3}{24}+\frac{2}{24}=\frac{5}{24}\ \text{tank/hour}\). Time \(=\frac{1}{\frac{5}{24}}=\boxed{\frac{24}{5}=4.8\ \text{hours}}\).
  \item Salt in original: \(0.25\times 200=50\ \text{mL}\). Let final volume be \(V\). Want \(10\%\): \(0.10V=50\Rightarrow V=500\ \text{mL}\). Water added \(=500-200=\boxed{300\ \text{mL}}\).
  \item Gross pay \(=38\times 14=\$532\). Deduction \(=0.12\times 532=\$63.84\). Take-home \(=532-63.84=\boxed{\$468.16}\).
  \item Scale factor \(=\frac{24}{16}=1.5\). Flour \(=2.5\times 1.5=3.75=\boxed{3\frac{3}{4}\ \text{cups}}\).
\end{enumerate}
