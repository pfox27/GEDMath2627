\section*{Study Tips}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Master unit conversions:} Practice converting between metric and imperial units regularly. Create flashcards for common conversions (cm to m, feet to yards, etc.).
    
    \item \textbf{Memorize key formulas:} Know the formulas for area, perimeter, volume, and circumference by heart. Understanding what each formula represents helps you apply it correctly.
    
    \item \textbf{Draw diagrams:} Always sketch the problem when dealing with geometry. Visual representations help you understand the problem better and reduce calculation errors.
    
    \item \textbf{Check your work:} After solving a problem, verify your answer by using a different method or plugging the answer back into the original equation.
    
    \item \textbf{Practice with real-world contexts:} Measurement problems often relate to practical situations. Connect abstract problems to real objects to deepen your understanding and retention.
\end{itemize}
